# Library and settings

## Keep your favorites together

Tap the heart on an anime's detail page to add or remove a favorite. Find saved titles in **Library → Favorites**.

**Library → History** lists recently watched anime. Where in-app playback is enabled, Anizuno saves episode progress so you can continue watching later. Downloads have their own Library tab in builds that enable them.

## Your library stays on your device

Favorites, watch history, progress, and preferences are stored locally. Anizuno does not currently provide account-based library sync or a backup-export feature. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove these records and downloaded episodes.

Use **Settings → Data & Storage → Clear Watch History** to clear the history list. Clearing history is separate from deleting downloads and does not remove saved favorites or all episode-progress records.

## Follow the schedule

Open **Schedule** and choose a day to see episodes grouped by airing time. Release information comes from external sources and may change.

To receive schedule reminders, turn on **Settings → Notifications → Schedule Alerts** and allow notification permission when your device asks. If you previously denied permission, enable it in your device's app notification settings.

Turn off Schedule Alerts to cancel scheduled reminders. Delivery also depends on your device's notification settings and operating system.

## Make it yours

- **Theme:** choose Light, Dark, Neo Brutalism, or follow your system.
- **Language:** choose English, Hindi, or your system preference. This changes supported interface text; it does not translate anime titles or add audio and subtitle tracks.
- **Playback:** prefer Sub or Dub, ask each time, and choose a preferred quality where playback is available.

The optional **NSFW 18+** section is accessed from Settings in builds that provide it. It requires age confirmation and a device unlock. It is separate from the main discovery experience and is for adults only.

## Privacy and support

Local library storage does not mean the app sends no usage data. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for analytics, crash reporting, service requests, and your available controls.
